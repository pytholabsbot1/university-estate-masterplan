Key moves (from the precedent study)

  1. Arrival sequence: a gateway plaza with a fountain, then a palm-lined 100 ft boulevard with high-street shops, then a landmark roundabout. Precedent: Prince Gates, Kingsville.
  2. Two addresses from one gate: the university and the gated residential enclave branch off the same roundabout, so campus traffic never enters the homes. Precedent: DLF Garden City institutional sector, Educomp Raffles.
  3. Green spine: a continuous 20 m greenway runs the length of the enclave, and the 500 and 400 sq yd plots back onto it. Precedent: Myscape, Springfield, Radburn.
  4. Plot gradient: the larger 500 and 400 sq yd plots are on the west, facing the campus and the hill range. The 250 sq yd courtyard plots are on the east and suit faculty rentals.
  5. Central park, lake and clubhouse at the heart, with park-facing plots on both sides. Precedent: Sanctuary Villas, Sattva.
  6. 60 ft tree-lined main avenue and quiet 40 ft streets with short blocks. Every plot faces east or west.
  7. Perimeter woodland belt with a jogging and cycling trail, plus themed gardens and a fruit tree on every plot. Precedent: Godrej Orchard Estate, Tangled Up in Green.
  8. Utilities: underground services, a sewage treatment plant and rainwater recharge pits in the greenway.
Concept stage. Plots are laid out at full size (500 sq yd = 50 × 90 ft; 400 sq yd = 40 × 90 ft; 250 sq yd = 30 × 75 ft) and counted. The 30/30/40 mix is by number of plots. Every other area is measured from the plan at 0.5 m per pixel and scaled to the 28.40 ha traced site area. The campus layout is indicative only.
