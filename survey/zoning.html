Premium design features

  • Grand gateway plaza on the outside road: a gatehouse, a 3-lane entry and exit with boom barriers, and a water feature.
  • 100 ft (30.5 m) signature boulevard with a 6 m palm-lined central median, a cycle track and wide footpaths.
  • High-street commercial at the entrance, with boutique retail and cafés facing both the boulevard and the outside road, and parking at the front.
  • The boulevard ends at the clubhouse. Pool, gym, banquet hall and spa sit at the head of the central park.
  • Central park: lawns, an amphitheatre and a water body, with park-facing premium plots on the ring street around it.
  • Sports complex: cricket nets, tennis and pickleball courts, a football turf and a skating rink, next to the school.
  • 6 m perimeter tree belt with a jogging track (about 2.4 km loop) inside the boundary wall.
  • Hospital or clinic and a school placed near the gate, so outside traffic stays out of the residential area.
  • Utilities: underground cabling, rainwater recharge, a sewage treatment plant, CCTV, and plots facing north and south on east–west streets.
