# University Estate master plan

Concept master plan for a gated plotted township with a 25-acre university, Jot Sadruddin + Jot Dariya, Tehsil Pahari, District Deeg, Rajasthan. Site 28.40 ha, traced from BhuNaksha.

- `generator/` — `layout.js` builds the layout and the area statement; `master2.html` draws the A2 sheet; `render.js` exports PNG/PDF with Playwright (`node render.js out.png out.pdf`, any OS); `render.sh` is the older Windows/Chrome exporter; `run.js` prints the land-use numbers.
- `survey/` — parcel tracing scripts, outline, plot records and the map-vs-record check.
- `output/` — v4 plan: 285 plots = 1,08,600 sq yd = 9.08 ha = 49.7% of the 18.28 ha net township; sellable incl. 21 SCO lots and commercial hub 54.1%.
- `legacy/` — v2 and v3 plans and exports. v3 never drew or counted the 100 ft boulevard, so its 51% / 21.7% roads figures are not reliable.

Concept stage only. Verify against the Rajasthan township rules, a DGPS survey and a patwari demarcation before approval.
